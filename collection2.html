Collection 2

Ambered Love


It centers on the idea of “ambered love,” exploring an emotional expression that is inclusive, gentle, and free of judgment. Influenced by my parents’ teachings and the Buddhist idea of compassion, the project sees amber as a symbol of protection and preservation. It holds the small, fragile, and imperfect elements of nature, allowing traces of time to remain gently visible. Through material experiments with dried flowers, lace, and UV resin, the collection transforms decay, fragility, and imperfection into a beauty that can be cherished, presenting love and acceptance as something shaped and deepened over time.
